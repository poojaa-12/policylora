## FINRA 2210(d)(1)(A)
URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2210
Quote: Communications must be fair and balanced and must provide a sound basis for evaluating the facts. No member may omit any material fact if the omission would cause the communication to be misleading.

## FINRA 2210(d)(1)(B)
URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2210
Quote: No member may make any false, exaggerated, unwarranted, promissory or misleading statement or claim in any communication.

## FINRA 2210(d)(1)(D)
URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2210
Quote: Statements must be clear and not misleading in context, and must provide balanced treatment of risks and potential benefits.

## FINRA 2220
URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2220
Quote: Options communications must not use broad generalities and must reflect the risks of options transactions, including that certain strategies can lose more than the amount invested.
